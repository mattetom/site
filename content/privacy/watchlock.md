# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what **WatchLock**, a Wear OS app that locks your watch with one tap, does with your data.

## The short version

WatchLock does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your watch.

## What the app does on your watch

- **Device admin permission.** WatchLock asks to be turned on as a device administrator with a single policy: locking the screen (`force-lock`). It uses it only when you tap Lock on the app icon, tile or complication. It cannot read your data, wipe the watch, or see or change your PIN.
- **Screen lock status.** The setup screen checks whether a PIN or pattern is set, only to tell you whether locking will actually protect the watch. The PIN itself is never accessible to the app.
- Nothing else is read, saved or transmitted.

## Third parties

WatchLock does not include third-party SDKs that collect data. Google Play may collect information about installs and crashes under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); that data is handled by Google, not by WatchLock.

## Removing the app

Open WatchLock, tap **Turn off device admin**, then uninstall it as usual. Since no data is stored, there is nothing left to delete.

## Children

WatchLock is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
