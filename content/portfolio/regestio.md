Regestio is the product version of the [management platform I built for UNITRE Tradate](/portfolio/unitre-gestionale/) — a University of the Third Age running 100+ courses and over a thousand members per academic year. What started as a tailored solution proved general enough to serve any organization with the same model: courses, enrollments, membership fees, payments and communications.

## From project to product

The goal was to make the platform adoptable without a custom engagement each time:

- A dedicated landing page that explains the value for associations, Universities of the Third Age, cultural circles and training providers.
- Four subscription tiers sized by number of members — Small, Medium, Large — plus a custom "Ultra" plan for larger organizations.
- Online subscription via Stripe Checkout: a prospect can activate a monthly plan directly, with no manual step in between.
- A demo request flow for organizations that want a guided walkthrough first.

## What's included

Every plan ships the full platform: member registry, course catalog with online enrollment, waiting lists, payment tracking, automatic PDF documents (receipts, attendance sheets, registers), trip management, role-based access and a companion mobile app for members — all hosted, backed up and kept up to date.

The result is a single, proven codebase delivered as a turnkey subscription service, ready for the next association to adopt.
