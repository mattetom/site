Want Blink to arm itself when you leave?

GeoCam arms your Blink cameras when you leave home and disarms them when you get back. Free on one phone, for iOS and Android. Pro adds family presence and schedules.

Most guides to Blink geofencing quietly assume you have an iPhone, because the usual workaround is an Apple Shortcuts automation. On Android there is no built-in equivalent, so the question “how do I make Blink arm when I leave?” has a different answer. This page covers what actually works on Android phones in 2026.

Short answer: the Blink app cannot arm by location on any phone. On Android your realistic options are an IFTTT location applet, an Alexa routine that can only arm, Home Assistant, or a dedicated app such as GeoCam, which arms when you leave and disarms when you come back and is free on one phone.

Why Android users have fewer workarounds

The official Blink app offers schedules and manual arm and disarm, but no location trigger (more on why). iPhone owners can build a Shortcuts automation on “Leave” and “Arrive” that calls Blink’s shortcut actions. Android has no system-wide automation app that talks to Blink, so every option goes through another service or another app.

The options on Android

IFTTT

IFTTT has a Blink service with Arm system and Disarm system actions, and its Android app can use your phone’s location. Two applets (arm when you leave, disarm when you arrive) make a basic geofence. The limits: two applets are the whole free plan, each applet follows a single phone, and the applets stop working if Android restricts the IFTTT app in the background. Details in GeoCam vs IFTTT.

Alexa routines

The Alexa app on Android supports a location trigger, and a routine can arm Blink when you leave. It cannot bring you home, though: Blink states that you cannot disarm your system with Alexa Routines, and voice disarm needs a code. See why Alexa routines can’t disarm Blink.

Home Assistant

With a Home Assistant server, the Companion app on each Android phone reports presence and an automation can call the Blink integration. It is the most flexible route and the one with the most setup and upkeep. See GeoCam vs Home Assistant.

A dedicated app

GeoCam is an app for iPhone and Android that does one job: it arms Blink when you leave and disarms it when you come back. It registers your home area with Android’s own geofencing service, so it works even when you have not opened the app in days, and it talks to Blink directly, with no other service in between.

  • Free: automatic arm and disarm on one phone and one Blink network.
  • Pro (€3.99 a month or €39.99 a year, 7-day free trial): the whole family, so the system arms only when the last person leaves; schedules; single cameras on or off when you leave, arrive or at a set time.

Settings that keep geofencing reliable on Android

Android is careful about apps running in the background, and geofencing apps need two permissions to work while the phone is in your pocket:

  1. Location: “Allow all the time”. “Only while using the app” is not enough, because the transition happens while the app is closed. Find it under Settings, Apps, the app, Permissions, Location.
  2. Battery: “Unrestricted”. Some phones, especially Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus, stop background work for apps on “Optimised” or “Restricted” battery. Under Settings, Apps, the app, Battery, choose “Unrestricted”.

These two settings apply to any geofencing app, IFTTT included. If automatic arming stops after a while, check them first.

FAQ

Can the Blink app arm automatically when I leave on Android?

No. The Blink app has no location-based arming on Android or iPhone. You need IFTTT, Alexa, Home Assistant or a dedicated app.

Is there an Android equivalent of Apple Shortcuts for Blink?

Not built in. Android has no system automation app that can control Blink, which is why Android users rely on IFTTT, Alexa or a dedicated geofencing app.

Does geofencing drain the battery on Android?

Not noticeably. Geofencing uses Android’s event-based location service, which wakes the app only when you cross the boundary of the home area instead of tracking you continuously.

Does GeoCam work on Android?

Yes. GeoCam is on Google Play and on the App Store. Arm on leave and disarm on arrive are free on one phone.
