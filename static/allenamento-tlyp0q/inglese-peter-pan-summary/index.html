Peter Pan: the story

J.M. Barrie, Peter Pan, Step by Step, Step 1 (Liberty). Summary in the words of the book.

Key points

  • Peter Pan is a small boy who never wants to grow up.
  • He lives on the island of Neverland with the Lost Boys and Tinker Bell, a very small fairy.
  • Wendy, John and Michael Darling fly with him to Neverland.
  • Captain James Hook and the pirates are looking for Peter. Hook is afraid of the crocodile.
  • At the end the children go home, but Peter doesn't want to be a man.

Chapter 1: Wake up! Peter Pan is here

All children grow up. The Darlings live at house number 14 in London. Wendy is their first child, and then they have two boys, John and Michael. The children's nurse is not a lady but a big dog. Her name is Nana.

One night the window of the nursery opens and a boy falls on the floor: he is Peter Pan. Nana jumps at the boy. Peter flies out of the window, but Nana catches the boy's shadow in her mouth. Mrs. Darling puts the shadow in a chest of drawers.

On Friday evening Mr. and Mrs. Darling go to a party. Then a light flies into the room. It is not really a light, but a very small fairy: Tinker Bell. Then Peter comes. "Tink, where is my shadow?" Peter finds his shadow, but he can't stick it on his body. He sits on the floor and cries. Wendy wakes up and sews the shadow on Peter's foot.

Peter lives with the Lost Boys in Neverland. He comes to Wendy's window to listen to her mother's stories. "Wendy, come with me and tell your stories to the other boys. I can teach you to fly, and you can be our mother."

Chapter 2: I am flying!

The children try to fly, but they can't. "I have fairy dust," says Peter. He blows it on the children. "I am flying!" says Michael. They all fly into the night. Nana barks and runs to the party. Mr. and Mrs. Darling go home: there are four shadows flying in the nursery.

To find Neverland, the children fly over the sea and in the clouds for many days. The pirates see Tink's light and fire a cannon at them.

There are six Lost Boys: Tootles, Nibs, Slightly, Curly and two small boys. The pirates (Cecco, Bill Jukes, Starkey, Smee) are looking for the Lost Boys. Captain Hook has curly black hair, sad blue eyes and a big hook on his arm. The Indians, with their beautiful princess Tiger Lily, are looking for the pirates. The last animal is a big crocodile.

The Lost Boys live in their Home Under the Ground. Every child needs the correct tree to go down into the home. "I have no arm because I fight with Peter! A crocodile has it now," says Hook. The crocodile has a clock in it: TICK TICK TICK. When Hook hears it, he runs away.

Chapter 3: Can she be our mother too?

Tinker Bell doesn't like Wendy. "Peter wants you to shoot the Wendy bird," she tells the boys. Tootles hits Wendy with his arrow and she falls to the ground. Peter is very angry. Then Wendy moves: she is OK! The boys make a small house for her, with windows, a chimney and beautiful flowers. Wendy cooks, cleans, sews their pants and socks, and tells stories.

One day a pirate boat arrives at Marooners' Rock, in the lagoon, with Smee, Starkey and Tiger Lily. "Those boys have a mother," says Hook. "Can she be our mother too?" asks Smee. "Good idea. The boys can walk the plank, and she can be our mother." Wendy says: "Never!" The boys and the pirates fight. Tiger Lily runs away. Hook hits Peter with his hook, and Peter is hurt. Then Hook sees the crocodile and swims to the ship.

The water is rising. Peter can't help Wendy, but he sees a kite: Wendy flies away with it. Now only Peter is on the rock.

Chapter 4: Do the children go home?

The Never Bird gives her nest to Peter. Peter puts her two eggs in a pirate's hat, gets into the nest, makes his shirt into a sail and goes home. Now the Indians and the Lost Boys are friends.

Wendy tells the boys a story about the Darling family. "All mothers love their children and keep the window open for them." Peter is upset: "My mother doesn't keep the window open for me. There is a new little boy in my bed." "Wendy, let's go home," cry John and Michael. The Lost Boys want to go too. But Peter says: "I am not going with you, Wendy. I want to be a little boy and to have fun."

That night Hook surprises the Indians, and Hook wins the fight. "The Indians hit their drum when they win a fight," says Peter. But the pirates have the drum, and Smee hits it!

Chapter 5: Do you believe in fairies?

The boys go up their trees and into the arms of the pirates. The pirates tie the children and take them to their ship, the Jolly Roger. Hook goes down Slightly's tree and puts yellow poison in Peter's cup.

"Hook's poison is in your cup," says Tink. Peter starts to take his medicine, but Tink drinks it. "It is poison, and now I am dying." When children believe in fairies, fairies get better. So Peter asks the children who are dreaming: "Do you believe in fairies? Then clap your hands." Many children clap. Tink is OK!

On the Jolly Roger, Smee ties Wendy to the mast. Then Hook hears TICK TICK TICK. "Oh no! Hide me!" But it isn't the crocodile: it is clever Peter! In the ship's cabin Peter opens the children's chains, then he cuts Wendy's rope. A big fight starts. "So, Pan, you are a cocky young boy." "Come and get me." Hook knows he can't win and jumps into the sea. The crocodile is waiting for him and swallows Captain James Hook.

Chapter 6: I don't want to be a man

The boys are wearing pirate clothes, and Captain Pan turns the ship to land. In London Mrs. Darling is sad, but the window is open for the children. The children come home and get into their beds. Mrs. Darling thinks she is dreaming, then she opens her arms and the children run to her. Mrs. Darling wants six new children, and Mr. Darling wants the boys too!

Mrs. Darling asks Peter to live with them too. "Do I need to go to school? And then go to an office? And then grow up into a man? I don't want to go to school, or to be a man." Peter can live with Tink in Wendy's Neverland house, in the tree tops. Wendy can go there in the spring, to help him clean.

Wendy grows up and gets married. Her daughter, little Jane, loves stories about Peter. One spring day Peter comes back. "I can't come," says Wendy, "I don't know how to fly. I am a woman now." Peter sits on the floor and cries. Then Jane and Peter fly away into the night. Jane grows up and has a daughter, Margaret. And the story goes on.

Questions

  1. Who is Nana?
  2. What does Wendy sew on Peter's foot?
  3. Who hits Wendy with an arrow?
  4. How does Peter go home from Marooners' Rock?
  5. What happens to Captain Hook at the end?
Show the answers (Mostra le risposte)

Answers: 1. She is the children's nurse: a big dog. 2. His shadow. 3. Tootles. 4. In the Never Bird's nest, with his shirt as a sail. 5. He jumps into the sea and the crocodile swallows him.
