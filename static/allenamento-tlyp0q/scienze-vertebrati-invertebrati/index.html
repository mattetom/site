Vertebrates and invertebrates

Subject: Science Date: 2026-10-14 (topic in the register), worksheets photographed on 15/11/2026

Key points

  • Vertebrates have a backbone and an internal skeleton that supports their body.
  • Invertebrates do not have a backbone. Some have a soft body, others have a hard outer covering, the exoskeleton.
  • There are 5 groups of vertebrates: fish, amphibians, reptiles, mammals, birds.
  • We studied 5 groups of invertebrates: molluscs, arachnids, insects, crustaceans, worms.
  • Leg trick: insect = 6 legs, arachnid = 8 legs.

The 5 groups of vertebrates

  • Fish: they live in water, breathe through gills, have fins and most have scales. Examples: salmon, tuna, shark.
  • Amphibians: they live both in water and on land. Young amphibians breathe through gills, adults through lungs and skin. Examples: frog, toad, salamander.
  • Reptiles: dry skin with scales, they breathe through lungs. Examples: snake, lizard, turtle, crocodile.
  • Mammals: they have hair or fur, and the mother feeds her babies with milk. Examples: human, dog, cat, dolphin.
  • Birds: they have feathers, wings and a beak. Many can fly, some cannot, like the penguin and the ostrich. Examples: eagle, robin.

The 5 groups of invertebrates

  • Molluscs: soft body, often with a shell. Snail, clam, octopus.
  • Arachnids: body in 2 parts, 8 legs, no wings and no antennae. Spider, scorpion.
  • Insects: body in 3 parts (head, thorax, abdomen), 6 legs, 2 antennae, many have wings. Bee, ant, ladybird.
  • Crustaceans: hard exoskeleton, many jointed legs, most live in water. Crab, lobster, shrimp.
  • Worms: soft, long body, sometimes divided into segments. They crawl. Earthworm, leech.

Remember: the jellyfish is an invertebrate too, like in the X-ray activity in your notebook.

Check questions

  1. What do vertebrates have that invertebrates do not have?
  2. What are the 5 groups of vertebrates?
  3. How many legs does an insect have? And a spider?
  4. Is a crab a vertebrate or an invertebrate? Which group?
  5. Are insects a group of vertebrates?
Show the answers

Answers: 1. A backbone. 2. Fish, amphibians, reptiles, mammals, birds. 3. An insect has 6, a spider has 8. 4. An invertebrate, a crustacean. 5. No, insects are invertebrates.
