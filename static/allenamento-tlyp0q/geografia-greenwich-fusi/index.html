GMT and time zones

Subject: Geography Date: 2026-09-24

Key points

  • In 1884 the countries of the world decided that the meridian at 0° passes through the Royal Observatory in Greenwich, near London.
  • The Earth rotates once every 24 hours, so it is divided into 24 time zones.
  • GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is the time in Greenwich: it is time zero, and we count all the other times from it.
  • Going east you add one hour every 15 degrees. Going west you subtract one hour.
  • Rome = GMT+1, Tokyo = GMT+9, New York = GMT-5, London = GMT.

Explanation

In Greenwich there is a metal line on the floor of the Observatory (at night sometimes a laser) that shows the meridian. You can stand with one foot in the Eastern Hemisphere and one foot in the Western Hemisphere. There is also a museum about time, navigation and astronomy.

The Earth rotates and turns all the way round in 24 hours. So when it is day in one country, it is night in another. We need a starting point: the time in Greenwich, GMT. When it is noon in Greenwich, it is 12:00 GMT.

Every country says how many hours it is ahead of or behind Greenwich:

  • GMT+3 means 3 hours ahead of GMT. The country is to the east.
  • GMT-5 means 5 hours behind GMT. The country is to the west.

How to do the sum. Start from the time in Greenwich and use the number:

  • Rome is GMT+1: if it is 12:00 in Greenwich, in Rome it is 12 + 1 = 13:00.
  • Tokyo is GMT+9: 12 + 9 = 21:00, that is 9 pm (in the evening, not am!).
  • New York is GMT-5: 12 - 5 = 7:00 in the morning (7 am). With minus you subtract, you do not add.

In real life time zones do not follow the meridians exactly: countries change them to follow their borders, and some use daylight saving time for part of the year.

Words to remember

Check questions

  1. Why is the Earth divided into 24 time zones?
  2. If you go east, do you add or subtract hours?
  3. It is 12:00 in Greenwich. What time is it in Rome (GMT+1)?
  4. It is 12:00 in Greenwich. What time is it in New York (GMT-5)?
  5. In which year did they decide that the meridian 0° passes through Greenwich?
Show the answers

Answers: 1. Because the Earth rotates once every 24 hours. 2. You add. 3. 13:00. 4. 7:00 in the morning, 7 am (12 - 5). 5. In 1884.
