Animal reproduction

Subject: Science Date: worksheets photographed on 15/11/2026 (no date)

Key points

  • Animals can be divided into 3 groups by how they reproduce.
  • Oviparous: the female lays eggs. The young grow inside the egg, outside the mother.
  • Ovoviviparous: the eggs stay inside the female's body while the young develop.
  • Viviparous: the young grow inside the mother, with no egg, and are born alive.

Explanation

Oviparous animals. The female lays eggs. The young develop inside the eggs and hatch when they are ready. Examples: birds (the hen), frogs, fish, turtles, insects (the butterfly).

Ovoviviparous animals. There is an egg, but it does not come out at once: it stays inside the mother while the young one grows. The egg hatches inside the mother or just after it is laid. Examples: some sharks, some snakes, some rays, the guppy (a small fish).

Viviparous animals. No egg: the young one grows inside the mother and is born alive. In mammals the mother feeds her baby with milk and looks after it until it is independent. Examples: humans, dogs, cats, elephants, horses, dolphins.

A trick to remember: ovo = egg, vivi = alive. Ovo-vivi-parous has both: there is an egg, but the baby comes out almost alive.

Check questions

  1. What does the female of an oviparous animal do?
  2. Where do the eggs stay in ovoviviparous animals?
  3. Is a dolphin oviparous, ovoviviparous or viviparous?
  4. Give an example of an ovoviviparous animal.
  5. True or false: "In ovoviviparous animals the eggs develop inside the female body".
Show the answers

Answers: 1. It lays eggs. 2. Inside the female's body. 3. Viviparous. 4. The guppy (or some sharks, some snakes, some rays). 5. True.
