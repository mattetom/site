Animal nutrition

Subject: Science Date: worksheets photographed on 15/11/2026 (no date)

Key points

  • We can also divide animals by what they eat (type of nutrition).
  • A herbivore eats plants. A carnivore eats meat.
  • An omnivore eats plants and animals.
  • An insectivore eats insects. A granivore eats seeds.
  • A scavenger feeds on dead animals.

Explanation

The name tells you what the animal eats. "Insecti-vore" eats insects, "grani-vore" eats grains, that is seeds. "Vore" comes from Latin and means "that eats".

Type What it eats Examples from the worksheets
herbivore plants, grass cow
carnivore meat lion
omnivore plants and animals human, bear, dog, pig
insectivore insects anteater, swallow
granivore seeds sparrow
scavenger dead animals vulture

Link with the food chain: herbivores are usually primary consumers; carnivores and omnivores are secondary consumers.

Check questions

  1. What do we call an animal that eats seeds?
  2. A vulture feeds on dead animals: what type of animal is it?
  3. Give an example of an omnivore.
  4. A swallow eats insects: is it an insectivore or a granivore?
Show the answers

Answers: 1. A granivore. 2. A scavenger. 3. A human, a bear, a dog or a pig. 4. An insectivore.
