Climate zones

Subject: Geography Date: 2026-10-01

Key points

  • There are 5 main parallels: Arctic Circle, Tropic of Cancer, Equator, Tropic of Capricorn, Antarctic Circle.
  • These parallels are the boundaries of the climate zones.
  • Between the two Tropics there is the Tropical (Equatorial) Zone: it is hot all year.
  • Between a Tropic and a Polar Circle there is a Temperate Zone: it has 4 seasons. Italy is here.
  • Beyond the Polar Circles there are the Polar Zones: they are cold all year.

Explanation

The climate of a place depends a lot on its latitude: how far it is from the Equator. Near the Equator the Sun heats more; near the poles it heats less.

The 5 main parallels, from top to bottom:

  1. Arctic Circle, 66.5° North: the boundary of the north Polar Zone.
  2. Tropic of Cancer, 23.5° North: the north limit of the Tropical Zone.
  3. Equator, 0°: it divides the Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.
  4. Tropic of Capricorn, 23.5° South: the south limit of the Tropical Zone.
  5. Antarctic Circle, 66.5° South: the boundary of the south Polar Zone.

So there are three kinds of climate zones:

  • the Tropical (Equatorial) Zone, between the two Tropics: the hottest.
  • the Temperate Zones, two of them: one between the Tropic of Cancer and the Arctic Circle, the other between the Tropic of Capricorn and the Antarctic Circle. They have four seasons.
  • the Polar Zones, two of them: north of the Arctic Circle and south of the Antarctic Circle. The coldest.

Be careful: the Equator is a line, not a zone. If the question is "Which zone is the hottest?", the answer is "the Tropical (Equatorial) Zone", not "the Equator".

Why are there seasons in the Temperate Zones? Because the Earth goes round the Sun in one year (its revolution) and its axis is tilted. So for part of the year our hemisphere gets more light (summer) and for another part it gets less (winter). You need both things: the journey round the Sun and the tilted axis.

Show the answers

Answers: 1. Arctic Circle, Tropic of Cancer, Equator, Tropic of Capricorn, Antarctic Circle. 2. The Tropical (Equatorial) Zone. 3. The north Temperate Zone. 4. The Temperate Zone. 5. Because the Earth goes round the Sun (revolution) and its axis is tilted.
