Vertebrates at a glance

Subject: Science Date: 2026-11-15 (from the worksheets of October and November)

At a glance

  • VERTEBRATES (with a backbone)
    • WARM-BLOODED
      • MAMMALS
      • BIRDS
    • COLD-BLOODED
      • REPTILES
      • AMPHIBIANS
      • FISH

Table

Group Body covered with Breathes with Blood How they are born Example
fish scales gills cold most from eggs (some ovoviviparous: guppy, some sharks) salmon
amphibians bare, wet skin young gills, adults lungs and skin cold eggs in water frog
reptiles dry skin with scales lungs cold most from eggs (some snakes are ovoviviparous) snake
mammals hair or fur lungs warm most born alive, then milk dog
birds feathers lungs warm eggs eagle

Insects (invertebrates) breathe through spiracles connected to tracheae.

Remember

  • Warm-blooded: mammals and birds. All the other vertebrates are cold-blooded.
  • Adult amphibians: lungs AND skin, never only one of the two.
  • Oviparous = lay eggs · ovoviviparous = eggs inside the female · viviparous = born alive.
  • Insects are invertebrates: they are not a group of vertebrates.
