Science review: living things and animals

Biology · The 5 kingdoms · Vertebrates and invertebrates
Science · Grade 4
Review sheet

1. Biology is the science that studies living things. The word comes from Greek: bios = life, logos = study. Like ecology: oikos + logos.

2. A living thing is born, grows, feeds, breathes, reproduces and dies. A rock does not.

3. Scientists divide living things into 5 kingdoms.

4. Animals are divided into vertebrates, which have a backbone, and invertebrates, which do not have a backbone.

5. There are many more invertebrates: about 95 animals out of 100 are invertebrates.

The 5 kingdoms

KingdomWhat are they like?Examples
Bacteria (Monera)very, very small, only one cell: you see them only with a microscopebacteria
Protistsvery small, most have one cell, they live in wateramoeba, many algae, phytoplankton

Plantsthey make their own food with sunlight: they are producerstree, grass, flower
Animalsthey eat other living things and they move: they are consumersfox, fish, spider

Vertebrates: 5 groups

GroupHow do you recognise them?Examples
Fishthey live in water, they have scales, gills and finsshark, tuna
Amphibianswet skin; young ones live in water and breathe with gills, adults can live on land and breathe with lungsfrog, newt
Reptilesdry skin with scales, they lay eggs on landsnake, lizard, turtle
Birdsfeathers, a beak and wings, they lay eggseagle, penguin
Mammalshair or fur, the babies drink their mother's milk, they breathe with lungsdog, whale, bat, human

Invertebrates: some groups

GroupHow do you recognise them?Examples
Insects6 legs, a body in 3 parts, often wingsant, bee, butterfly
Arachnids8 legsspider, scorpion
Crustaceansa hard shell, many legs, most live in watercrab, shrimp, krill
Molluscsa soft body, often with a shellsnail, octopus
Wormsa long, soft body with no legsearthworm

Other invertebrates in the sea: jellyfish, coral, sea star.

Be careful!

A whale and a dolphin are mammals, not fish. A bat is a mammal, not a bird. A penguin is a bird, even if it cannot fly.

A spider is not an insect: it has 8 legs. An octopus is an invertebrate. Fungi are not plants.
